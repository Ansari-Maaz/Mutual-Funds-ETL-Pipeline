# Core Europe outside scope: AT BE LI MT

**Austria**
- OeKB (Oesterreichische Kontrollbank): collects data for all Austrian funds; current fund prices free; tax reporting-fund lists on my.oekb free; fund measures free without registration; FondsdatenPortal (FundsXML, structured portfolio/holdings) needs postal registration and per-download fees (2026 price list PDF). NEW. — [OeKB price data](https://www.oekb.at/en/capital-market-services/our-range-of-data-knowledge-creates-an-advantage/funds/price-data.html), [fund data portal](https://www.oekb.at/en/capital-market-services/our-range-of-data-knowledge-creates-an-advantage/funds/fund-data-portal.html), [fees](https://www.oekb.at/dam/jcr:f1879546-1361-4935-ac7c-af9d024321e1/Anlage-5_OeKB-FondsdatenPortal-Entgelte_20260101.pdf)
- FMA Austria official fund list: not found.

**Belgium**
- FSMA data portal holds lists of enrolled/authorised/registered entities incl. funds with statute and home country; quarterly NAV overview PDFs of Belgian public funds (aggregate). BEAMA/Febelfin quarterly aggregate figures. No bulk NAV dataset found. NEW. — [FSMA UCI](https://www.fsma.be/en/undertaking-collective-investment-uci), [data portal](https://www.fsma.be/en/data-portal)

**Liechtenstein**
- FMA-LI register of investment undertakings plus quarterly PDF lists ("Liste Finanzmarktteilnehmer", Q2 2025 seen) and quarterly Mutationslisten (new AIF/UCITS with management company, date). No NAV. NEW. — [register](https://www.fma-li.li/en/financial-intermediaries/asset-management-and-markets-division/investment-undertakings-under-the-iua-and-their-management-companies/register), [Q3 2025 change list](https://www.fma-li.li/fma-li/documents/standort/20250930_fma-mutationsliste_q3_2025.pdf)
