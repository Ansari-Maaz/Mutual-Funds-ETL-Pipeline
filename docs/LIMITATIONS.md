# Limitations

- The dated local evidence snapshot (2026-10-28) records 51 registered source rows and 30 sources marked integrated. Country-level record counts are in the [coverage table](../README.md#country-and-market-coverage). Do not treat this local snapshot as the current production database.
- That snapshot has fund records in 18 of 24 target markets. Since it was recorded, Japan and South Korea source connectors have been added. Japan imports NISA-eligible domestic fund subsets; South Korea imports products listed by IBK and leaves domicile null because the source does not report it. Singapore, Taiwan, Russia, and the UAE still have no imported fund rows in that cited snapshot. Luxembourg, Ireland, and South Africa are expansion markets; Spain is additional to target scope.
- The five Banco de España country-list imports cover open-ended non-monetary investment fund products in DE, IT, NL, DK and LU. Those registers include both UCITS and AIFs, so they are broader than a strict UCITS-only mutual-fund universe. They publish no currency or NAV.
- The Hong Kong SFC source lists funds authorized for public offering in Hong Kong, including overseas-domiciled products. Domicile is therefore left null; this dataset represents market availability, not a Hong Kong-domiciled universe.
- Brazil's CVM connector ingests a selected month or an explicitly requested range of up to 12 monthly archives. An all-history backfill is not performed by default.
- Brazil's official CVM CDA archive is the structured portfolio-holdings source; it can omit confidential positions and its CSV field layout varies across archive groups.
- France's AMF GECO registry provides fund/share-class identities and documents, but no central structured portfolio-holdings feed. French holdings require issuer-specific report adapters and are not represented as complete national coverage.
- GSFM's combined price export has no currency column. The connector loads only rows explicitly labelled `(AUD)`; reported Australian fund and NAV coverage is in the dated coverage snapshot.
- CSRC, ASISA and FINMA snapshots do not provide NAV or currency in the fields used here. The project preserves null values and does not manufacture these fields.
- Taiwan's government catalog documents daily NAV and currency, but the publisher CSV host timed out during repeated attempts. No Taiwan rows were imported.
- Japan's IMAJ and FSA files cover NISA-eligible funds and a monthly new-fund subset, not the entire Japanese fund universe. The broad IMAJ search library prohibits automated scraping. No Japanese NAV feed is included in these imports.
- South Korea's IBK CSV covers products distributed by that bank, not all Korean funds or a verified domicile universe. It contains performance percentages and fees but no NAV, currency, or domicile. KOFIA has public individual-fund search; no complete public bulk feed was verified.
- U.S. coverage is a whole-universe view, not a claim of complete data. As of the latest audit, about 65% of U.S. domiciled fund records have at least one NAV observation and about 82% have portfolio holdings. The remaining gaps are concentrated in share classes without a ticker and in funds that do not file N-PORT.
- U.S. holdings come from official SEC Form N-PORT filings. Series rows are duplicated across matched share classes, and `PERCENTAGE` is converted from a fraction to a percentage. Money-market funds file N-MFP instead of N-PORT; `sync-sec-nmfp-bulk` imports their monthly portfolio schedule and fills manager/administrator fields. Its class NAV table has no currency column, so canonical USD observations are written only when `--assign-usd-nav` is requested for U.S. domiciled funds.
- Replaying older N-PORT quarters (2026q2, 2026q1, 2025q4) with `--only-missing` produced no additional matches. The remaining N-PORT holdings gaps are therefore not older filings that were skipped; they are series that did not file in those quarters (inactive, pre-launch, or non-N-PORT filers).
- U.S. NAV is imported only from official issuer pages or regulator data. Issuer sites that block automation or render NAV client-side (for example Fidelity, John Hancock, MassMutual, Invesco, Schwab, Nationwide, Direxion, Themes, Innovator, ProShares and Tidal) remain uncovered instead of being replaced with unofficial prices. Yahoo and Nasdaq price series are not treated as verified official NAV.
- About 9,600 U.S. records have no ticker, mostly variable-insurance trusts, and no centralized official daily NAV source exists for them. They stay without NAV rather than being matched to a different share class.
- Capital Group's adapter only reads retail product pages; 529 share classes without one are not covered.
- Raw snapshots are intentionally excluded from Git. Use [RAW_SNAPSHOTS.md](RAW_SNAPSHOTS.md) to retrieve file-based data and write source URL, retrieval time, and SHA-256 provenance manifests.
- Commercial reuse rights have not been reviewed for all providers. Check each provider's terms before redistribution or commercial use.
- PostgreSQL 16 runs in the project's persistent Docker container on port 5433.
